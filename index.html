How to read this

Every forecast day is a breakdown day: the bars show how likely it is to be electrical, mechanical or other (utility, warehouse, process and unclassified stops). Days with no breakdown are not modelled. For electrical and mechanical, the two most likely equipment families are shown; for “other” no reason is given. The type shares follow each mill’s recent mix, so treat them as a rough lean, not a firm call.
