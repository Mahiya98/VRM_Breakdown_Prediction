How to read this

Each equipment family has a typical interval between breakdowns. When the current gap exceeds the 75th-percentile interval, that family is flagged OVERDUE — it has gone longer than usual without a breakdown and deserves inspection. The risk score is how many times past the typical gap: 2.0× means twice as long as usual. Equipment families are ranked by urgency. The equipment forecast (next 5 days) ranks the three most likely families if an electrical or mechanical breakdown occurs, along with the most likely cause for each equipment (e.g. vibration, jam, temperature).
